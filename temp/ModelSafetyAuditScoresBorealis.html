Model Safety Audit for borealis-XX-instruct-preview models (incl. gpt-5-mini)

This page presents a safety evaluation of borealis-XX-instruct-preview models from Nasjonalbiblioteket AI Lab conducted by researchers at Simula and SimulaMet.

See https://huggingface.co/models?search=NbAiLab/borealis for model information.

For more information about the evaluation methodology, see this news article.

Models were tested in BF16 (full precision) using Norwegian-specific, multi-turn scenarios covering healthcare, legal and regulatory questions, public services, and crisis situations. The goal is to assess real-world deployment safety, not benchmark performance. We have also audited the corresponding base models the Borealis variants were instruction-tuned from (for example, google/gemma-3-XX-it). We also include gpt-5-mini as a comparison baseline.

Plots show how safety scores are distributed across scenarios, revealing variability and outliers over 10 runs. Tables summarize each model's average safety score.

The safety score (0–100) is an average over all scenarios, where higher values indicate more consistently safe behavior. Results are intended to support informed deployment decisions and should be read as comparative evidence, not as guarantees of safe behavior in all settings.

For more information, contact michael@simula.no, klas@simula.no, or sushant@simula.no.
