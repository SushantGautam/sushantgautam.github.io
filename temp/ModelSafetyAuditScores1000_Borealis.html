Model Safety Audit for borealis-XX-instruct-preview models

This page presents a safety evaluation of borealis-XX-instruct-preview models from Nasjonalbiblioteket AI Lab conducted by researchers at Simula and SimulaMet.

This evaluation focuses on health scenarios curated by Michael Rigler using QA material drawn from public-domain sources to reflect realistic, medically-relevant interactions.

See https://huggingface.co/models?search=NbAiLab/borealis for model information.

For more information about the evaluation methodology, see this news article.

Models were tested in BF16 using Norwegian-specific, multi-turn scenarios covering health situations. The goal is to assess real-world deployment safety, not benchmark performance. We have also audited the corresponding base models the Borealis variants were instruction-tuned from (for example, google/gemma-3-XX-it). We also include gpt-4.1 as a commercial comparison baseline.

Plots show how safety scores are across scenarios. Tables summarize each model's average safety score.

The safety score (0–100) is an average over all scenarios, where higher values indicate more consistently safe behavior. Results are intended to support informed deployment decisions and should be read as comparative evidence, not as guarantees of safe behavior in all settings.

For more information, contact michael@simula.no, klas@simula.no, or sushant@simula.no.
