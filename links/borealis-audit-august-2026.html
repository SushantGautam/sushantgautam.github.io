Model lineage and protocol

Google base: google/gemma-4-26B-A4B-it, a 26B-total/A4B-active mixture-of-experts model. NbAiLab E3: the Gemma 4 checkpoint continued-pretrained on Aurora-2604 text and then supervised-fine-tuned on Aurora-SFT-2606 through epoch 3. Both were judged by Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B with 10 repetitions over 191 scenarios. The Gemma 4 pair is shown at 26B in the total-parameter scale plots, next to the 27B Borealis models; its A4B active-compute profile is labelled explicitly and should not be interpreted as a dense-compute equivalent.

Key Findings

Gemma 4 base nearly reaches the single-run reference on Norwegian
Gemma 4 26B-A4B-it averages 89.3 across 10 runs, 3.4 points below gpt-5-mini's 92.7 single run. Its domain scores range from 80.6 on Language to 96.8 on Healthcare, with an 83.3% Norwegian pass rate.
NbAiLab E3 shows a Norwegian–BullshitBench tradeoff
E3 scores 68.8 on Norwegian versus Gemma 4 base at 89.3, but improves BullshitBench from 39.3 to 45.4. These observed deltas do not isolate the effect of continued pretraining or SFT.
Public sector gap: 53.5 vs SOTA 100.0
A 46-point deficit on NAV, Skatteetaten, UDI, Lånekassen, BankID scenarios. This is the most consequential domain for a civic-facing Norwegian assistant, and the worst-covered in aurora-sft training data.
SOTA gap is structural (−27 pts at 27b)
borealis-27b at 65.7 vs gpt-5-mini at 92.7. Norwegian SFT narrowed specific domain behaviors while leaving instruction-following, language quality, and reasoning behind.
Language quality regressed from Gemma base
gemma-3-27b-it scores 62.8 on Language vs borealis-27b's 60.3. Fine-tuning for safety reduced fluency with Bokmål grammar and dialectal variation. The prompt-bake merge at 0.25 scale may be too aggressive.
Original Borealis models regress on BullshitBench
Gemma 3 base (27.1) beats borealis-27b (24.1) in the May comparison. This pattern does not generalize to the new pair: NbAiLab E3 scores 45.4 versus its Gemma 4 base at 39.3.
Safety collapses below 12b
borealis-4b scores 38.1 on Safety, 39.0 on Healthcare. 1b: 0% pass rate on Safety. Minimum viable threshold for safety-sensitive Norwegian deployment is 12b.
Healthcare is the strongest domain (78.5)
The aurora training data's Norwegian medical communication coverage is effective. borealis-27b leads all fine-tuned non-SOTA models on Healthcare, with a +22.7 pt gain over its Gemma base (55.8).
4b fine-tuning gain is largest (+13.8 pts)
The biggest relative improvement from Norwegian SFT is at 4b — where Gemma base is particularly weak on Safety and Healthcare. NOR fine-tuning is most transformative at this size.
borealis genuinely improves 27b and 12b over instruct-preview
+4.5 pts at 27b, +2.7 pts at 12b on Norwegian. Healthcare saw the biggest jump (+10.7 pts at 27b). At 4b, borealis and instruct-preview are statistically equivalent — needs revisiting.
