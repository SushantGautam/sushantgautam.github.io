🎓 MADTRS Thesis

AI-Based Thumbnail Generation for Soccer Clips using Multi-Signal Analysis

A multi-stage AI pipeline that filters, analyzes and ranks frames from soccer goal clips to select thumbnail candidates. It combines camera shot-type classification, logo detection, face detection, emotion and pose signals, and image quality, with a GUI for running the pipeline and a user study comparing five thumbnail methods.
